First version:
- Plays music only from the folders you choose, never goes online
- FLAC, ALAC, AAC, MP3, Ogg and Opus, gapless, with ReplayGain
- Albums, artists, tracks and search, compilations included
- Playlists with import and export, and a Starred playlist
- Queue you can reorder, kept across restarts
