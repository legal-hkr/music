v0.1.0

First version:
- Plays music only from the folders you choose, never goes online
- FLAC, ALAC, AAC, MP3, Ogg and Opus, gapless, with ReplayGain
- Albums, artists, tracks, playlists and search, in the order you like
- Playlists with import and export, and Starred
- A queue with shuffle and repeat, kept across restarts
- Backup and restore of settings and playlists
