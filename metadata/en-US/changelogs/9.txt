v0.3.5

New:
- Apple Lossless (ALAC) plays on every phone, decoded by the app itself (GH #8)
- Unplayable tracks are skipped with a message; files in formats the phone doesn’t support are listed as skipped

Changes:
- On the Output page, the ReplayGain, DSD and equalizer steps open their settings; the equalizer button is no longer in the volume bar
- Buttons, dialogs and list icons now look like Android’s own
- The built-in Audeze iSine 10 equalizer preset has been removed
